Complete installation guide for Orion OS on the **Redmi Note 8/8T (Ginkgo)**. This guide walks you through a clean flash from start to finish.

> **Info:** Both GApps and Vanilla builds are available for Ginkgo. Choose the one that fits your setup.

## Before you start

- Charge your device to at least **50%**.
- Back up your data — flashing a ROM **will erase everything**.
- Have the Orion OS zip and your **firmware** ready on internal storage.

## Installation Steps

### Step 1: Install Recovery & Boot

1. Make sure **TWRP** or **OrangeFox** recovery is installed.
2. Power off the device.
3. Press and hold `Volume Up + Power` to boot into recovery.

### Step 2: Flash Firmware (if needed)

If you are coming from MIUI or another ROM, flash the latest firmware first:

1. Go to **Install** and flash the firmware zip.
2. Do **not** reboot yet.

### Step 3: Flash Orion OS

1. Go to **Install** and select the Orion OS ROM:
   - `OrionOS-16.0-Ozone-ginkgo-Official-xxx.zip`
2. Swipe to confirm the flash.

### Step 4: Format Data

> **Warning:** This erases all user data. Make sure your backup is safe.

1. Go to **Wipe → Format Data**.
2. Type `yes` to confirm.

### Step 5: Reboot

1. Reboot to **System**.
2. The first boot can take **5–10 minutes** — be patient.
3. Complete the setup wizard and you're ready to go.
