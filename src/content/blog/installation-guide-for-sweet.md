# Complete Installation Guide for Orion OS

Welcome to the comprehensive installation guide for Orion OS on **Redmi Note 10 Pro (Sweet)**. This guide provides a step-by-step approach to ensure a smooth transition to Orion OS.

---

> **Info:** Only GApps variants are supported for Sweet devices.

---

## Installation Steps

### Step 1: Prepare & Wipe
Clean your device partitions to avoid conflicts.

1. **Recovery:**
   - Ensure you have the latest **OrangeFox** recovery installed for Sweet.
2. **Boot into Recovery:**
   - Turn off your device.
   - Press and hold `Volume Up + Power` buttons.
3. **Execute Clean Wipe:**
   - Go to `Wipe` → `Custom Wipe`
   - Select: **System, Vendor, Dalvik, Cache, and Data**.
   - Swipe to confirm.

> **Warning:** This step completely erases your apps and settings. Ensure your backup is safe!

---

### Step 2: Flash Orion OS ROM
The core of your new experience.

1. Navigate to **Install**.
2. Flash latest Firmware for Sweet devices.
3. Select the Orion OS ROM file for Sweet:
   - `OrionOS-16.0-Ozone-sweet-Official-GApps-xxx.zip`
4. Swipe to flash and wait (2-5 minutes).

---

### Step 3: Format Data
This is the most critical step after a clean flash to prevent bootloops.

1. Go to **Wipe** menu.
2. Tap **Format Data** (not just Wipe Data).
3. Type `yes` to confirm.
4. Wait for the process to complete.

---

### Step 4: Final Reboot
You're almost there!

1. Go to **Reboot** → **System**.
2. **Initial Boot:** The first boot can take 5-10 minutes.
3. **Setup:** Follow the on-screen instructions to set up your new OS.
