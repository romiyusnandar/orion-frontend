Complete installation guide for Orion OS on the **Poco F5 (Marble)**. Follow these steps for a clean and stable install.

> **Info:** Marble ships with a GApps build for Android 16. A Vanilla build is also available for Android 15.

## Before you start

- Keep your device above **50% battery**.
- Back up your data — this process **wipes everything**.
- Download the ROM and matching **firmware** to internal storage.

## Installation Steps

### Step 1: Boot to Recovery

1. Ensure a custom recovery (TWRP / OrangeFox) is installed.
2. Power off, then hold `Volume Up + Power` to enter recovery.

### Step 2: Flash Firmware

1. From **Install**, flash the latest HyperOS firmware for Marble.
2. Stay in recovery — do not reboot yet.

### Step 3: Flash Orion OS

1. Go to **Install** and select:
   - `OrionOS-16.0-Ozone-marble-Official-GApps-xxx.zip`
2. Swipe to confirm. Wait for the flash to finish (2–5 minutes).

### Step 4: Format Data

> **Warning:** Formatting data removes all user content. Confirm your backup first.

1. Open **Wipe → Format Data**.
2. Type `yes` to confirm and wait for completion.

### Step 5: First Boot

1. Reboot to **System**.
2. Let the first boot finish (around **5–10 minutes**).
3. Run the setup wizard and explore your new ROM.
